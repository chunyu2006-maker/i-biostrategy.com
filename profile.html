Profile

Strategic leadership in life science innovation.

Innovate BioStrategy is an independent strategic advisory practice founded by Luming Niu, helping life science companies and investors transform scientific innovation into commercial value. The practice supports informed decision-making by evaluating market opportunities, developing product and commercial strategies, and communicating compelling product value.

Background

Luming Niu, M.D., brings more than 20 years of leadership experience across the life science and biotechnology industries. Her expertise spans product strategy, commercial strategy, commercialization, strategic partnerships, licensing, business development, and commercial opportunity assessment.

She has held leadership and strategic commercial roles at GeneCopoeia, Elsevier, Agilent Technologies, and other life science organizations, working at the intersection of science, business, and investment.

She advises executives, leadership teams, and investors seeking to evaluate opportunities, strengthen product and commercial strategy, differentiate their offerings, and translate scientific innovation into sustainable commercial success.
